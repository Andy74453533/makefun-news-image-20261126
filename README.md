A one-image summary of the world's news of Thursday 26 November 2026,
composed as a 1024x1024 newspaper front page and copied to `/output/entry.png`
by `main.ts` (Node 22).

Lead story: a fuel tanker overturned and exploded on Cairo's Ring Road in
Giza — 31 killed, 21 injured. Also in the image: the U.S. proposes new
tariffs on 14 trading partners (China up to 20%); a drone attack in Kuwait
kills two Italian technicians; and the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade in New
York is postponed by a storm, for the first time in 100 years.

The photographs are AI-drawn illustrations of the day's news (composed with
`compose.py` in this repo). Facts verified against Reuters and CNN reporting
of 26 November 2026.

Run it with:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
